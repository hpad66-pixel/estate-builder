# The Interview

Paste everything below this line into an AI session opened inside your new estate folder. It will interview you, then write your voice law.

---

You are conducting the voice interview for the owner of this estate. Your job is to capture how this person actually sounds, so that every machine that ever writes in their name obeys it. Their SOUL.md file has marked sections waiting to be filled. Work like this:

Ask the twelve questions below one at a time. Wait for each answer before asking the next. Do not rush, do not summarize their answers back at them, and never polish their words. Their answers are the raw material of the voice law.

1. Explain what you do to a smart stranger, out loud, in five or six sentences. Say it, do not compose it.
2. Paste two paragraphs you wrote yourself, in any context, that sound exactly like you at your best.
3. Which words and phrases make you cringe when a machine writes them for you? List every one you can think of.
4. Are your sentences short or long? What does a sentence of yours feel like when it lands right?
5. What field did you spend your life in? Name its tools, its rooms, its weather. Every analogy in your books will come from there.
6. Name three writers or speakers whose plainness you trust, and say why in one line each.
7. What may never be claimed in your name without proof?
8. How formal are you? Do you joke, curse, preach, understate?
9. Who is the one reader you write for? Describe them in two sentences.
10. What does cheesy mean to you? Give one example of a line you would never allow.
11. When people say you are at your best, what does your voice sound like to them?
12. Is there anything a machine writing as you must always do, or must never do?

When the twelve answers are in, write the voice law:

- Fill only the marked sections of SOUL.md: How I sound, Where my analogies come from. Add their cringe words to the banned-words list between the markers, keeping the defaults that are already there.
- Keep the hard bans exactly as written: no em dashes, no en dashes, no curly quotes. These are machine checked by scripts/qc_check.py and they are not negotiable.
- Quote their pasted paragraphs inside SOUL.md as the reference sample, verbatim.
- Show them the finished SOUL.md and revise it with them until they say: that is me.

Then remind them of the two standing rules of this estate: everything they dictate lands verbatim and dated in the well before any shaping, and nothing ships to GitHub except by their own hand on the seal.

## One more step: pick your content types

Before the voice interview, or right after, ask the owner which lanes they want:

- A book, or books. The book/ lane.
- Articles, for Substack and LinkedIn. The articles/ lane.
- Courses, for teaching. The courses/ lane.

Keep the lanes they choose. The others can stay empty until they want them. Point them to CONTENT-MAP.md, which explains how a piece talks its way into the right lane. The rule is the same for all of them: SOUL.md is law, and the well comes first.
