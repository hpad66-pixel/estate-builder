## The reframe

Lead with the reframe, not the background. The reader should know by the second
sentence why the thing they believed is incomplete. Do not warm up, and do not
explain what the piece is about. Be about it.

## The example

One real case, from the world the reader actually lives in. Named and specific
when the case is being praised. Anonymized when the case is struggling, because
critiquing a pattern is useful and naming a struggling organization is not.

Every claim carries its source. Anything uncertain carries a [VERIFY] flag and
stays flagged until it is checked against a primary source. A trade article
pointing at a number is not the number. Go to the filing, the rule, the agency
page, and quote that.

## The argument

This is where the piece earns its length. Plain sentences, one idea per
paragraph. Cut facts before you cut explanation. A reader can look up a number.
They cannot reconstruct your reasoning.

## The framework, if the material supports one

Give it a plain name a person can repeat out loud. A framework a reader can draw
on a napkin beats one with nine boxes. If the material does not support a
framework, skip this rather than inventing one.

## Takeaways

Three to five. Each a complete sentence. Each standing on its own if the reader
scrolled straight down to here.

## What to do about it

By seat. What the operator does, what the manager does, what the board does. An
article without next steps is a lecture.
