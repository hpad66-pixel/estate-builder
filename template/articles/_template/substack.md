# The Substack cut

Made from article.md. Not a separate draft. If a fact changes, it changes in the
master first and this gets remade.

## What changes from the master

Substack is the long home. Keep the full argument. What you trim is scaffolding,
not substance.

- Cut the section headings if the piece flows without them. A newsletter reads
  more like a letter than a report.
- Keep every source. Link them inline, in the sentence, rather than collecting
  them at the bottom where nobody goes.
- Keep the takeaways and the by-seat actions. They are the reason somebody
  forwards it to a colleague.
- Lead with the reframe in the first two sentences. The preview text in an inbox
  is short, and it decides whether the piece gets opened at all.

## The parts Substack needs that the master does not have

Title: under sixty characters if you can. Say the thing, do not tease it.

Subtitle: one sentence telling the reader what they will be able to do after
reading. This is the second half of the open decision.

Cover image: usually figure 01 from figures/. It doubles as the social card.

Pull quotes: two to four from the master's frontmatter, set as block quotes with
{{AUTHOR}} attributed underneath.

Footer: the standing line that turns readers into contributors. Keep it
identical across every issue so it becomes familiar rather than promotional.
