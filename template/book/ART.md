# ART.md · the sketchbook system

Figures are hand-drawn line work on warm paper, made as inline SVG in standalone HTML pages under book/figures/.

Palette: paper #f4efe5, panel #efe8da, ink #1a1a1a and #3a352d, muted #7c7566, plus three working colors chosen with the owner: one for the built and the observed, one for the loop and the lesson, one used sparingly for warnings and cost. Every figure uses the same three-color code so a reader learns it once.

Type: one hand-lettered face for labels (Caveat or similar), one serif for captions (Spectral or similar).

Line: apply a subtle roughening filter (feTurbulence plus feDisplacementMap) so lines read as drawn, not plotted.

Forms: a full-page bordered plate may open a chapter; inline figures sit beside the paragraph they serve. Every figure gets a caption in plain words, and no figure ships that the text does not earn.
