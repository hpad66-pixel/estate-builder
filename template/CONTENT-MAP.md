# CONTENT-MAP.md · what this estate holds and where it lands

{{AUTHOR}}'s estate can hold more than one kind of work. Pick the lanes you want at the interview. The ones you skip stay empty until you need them.

## The lanes

- book/ · long-form. Chapters, figures, the compiled book. The Scribe writes here.
- articles/ · short-form thought leadership for Substack, LinkedIn, a newsletter. One dated folder per piece. Every article is a seed that can grow into a chapter, a course lesson, or a talk.
- courses/ · teaching. Modules and lessons built from what you already wrote. Blueprint before prose.

## Wire it up: talk, and it lands in the right lane

Say it, or drop a document, a PDF, a talk, a dictation. State the destination in one line, then write it after your okay. What the thing is decides the lane.

- An idea or a post: articles/
- A chapter or a story: book/
- A lesson or a class: courses/
- A voice rule or a belief: SOUL.md, by promotion only, with your sign-off

The well comes first. Anything you dictate lands verbatim and dated in outputs/book-evidence/ before any shaping, whichever lane it ends up in.

## One voice across all of it

Every lane obeys SOUL.md. No exceptions, no matter the channel. Run scripts/qc_check.py before any piece is called done.
